title: The Bug Collector
keyword: Bugs
subject: Art
skills: Natural forms, Elements of art, Tertiary colours, Collage

Inspired by the framed displays of insect collectors, students designed their own collection of colourful beetles and bugs. They studied photographs of real insects, and some live ones too, then mixed their own tertiary colours to paint sheets of paper. Cutting these into shapes, they built each insect as a collage, learning how line, shape and colour work together to create bold designs.
