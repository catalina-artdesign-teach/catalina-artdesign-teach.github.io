title: Shape, Colour & Rhythm
keyword: Shape
skills: Colour mixing, Composition, Cut-paper collage, Gouache
school year: 2025-26

In this project students learn how colour and shape create rhythm and balance. They research the Bauhaus, mix primary, secondary and complementary colours in gouache, and build their own compositions through cut-paper collage. Along the way, they learn to explain their choices using specific art vocabulary.
