title: Dragons in Motion
keyword: Dragons
subject: Art
skills: Movement, Chinese art, Ink drawing, Calligraphy pens

As part of an art history unit, students inquired into ancient Chinese art, with movement as the big idea. They studied how dragons twist and flow through Chinese painting, then drew their own on tracing paper. Working with ink and calligraphy pens, they explored line, pattern and touches of red and gold to make their dragons feel alive and full of energy.
