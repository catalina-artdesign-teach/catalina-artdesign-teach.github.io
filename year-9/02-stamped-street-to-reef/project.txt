title: Stamped: From Street to Reef
keyword: Stamps
subject: Design
skills: Community, Stamp making, Colour layering, Repetition

In this exploratory unit, students looked at street art and stencils, and how designers use repetition and juxtaposition. They cut their own stamps from foam board and built compositions with one, two and three layers of colour. To finish, each student designed a stamp inspired by coral reefs, and together they printed one collaborative underwater scene. Like a real reef, it shows how many small parts can form one community.
