title: Stories in Blue
keyword: Cyanotype
subject: Art
skills: Storytelling, Cyanotype, Negative space, Composition

With storytelling as the starting point, students explored how contrast can make an image feel mysterious and magical, like a scene from a fairy tale. They arranged pressed plants and animal figures on light-sensitive paper and exposed them to the sun to make cyanotype prints. Working with negative space, they turned silhouettes into stories about nature, from a deer in the forest to a jellyfish in the sea.
