title: Kathunk!
keyword: Kathunk
subject: Art
skills: Communication, Lettering, Stamp printing, 3D collage

Following their study of Lichtenstein, students explored how Pop Art turns sounds into pictures. Each student chose an onomatopoeia, such as BOOM, SLASH or KATHUNK, and designed bold lettering that shows its meaning. They printed Ben-Day dots using bubble wrap, cut and layered coloured card, and built their words into dynamic 3D collages that seem to burst off the page.
