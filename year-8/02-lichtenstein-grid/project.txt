title: POW! Lichtenstein
keyword: Pop Art
subject: Art
skills: Popular culture, Pop Art, Grid method, Proportion

Exploring popular culture as the key concept, students investigated Pop Art and its links with comics, focusing on the work of Roy Lichtenstein. Each student chose one of his paintings and made an artist's study of it in markers, using the grid method to scale it up, much as Lichtenstein enlarged images from comic books. Along the way, they practised drawing accurately and in proportion.
