title: Hi, I'm Catalina

I'm a professional designer with a Master's in Education and 15 years of experience teaching in schools. I teach Art, Design and Computing, and I love them because they open up a different way of learning: messier, more divergent, more exciting and a lot more fun.

I'm a great believer in concept-based learning, so my lessons are built around big ideas that students can connect and carry into new projects. I make sure every student feels included, and together we discover and celebrate artists and designers from many different cultures. Everything on this site was made by my students, and I'm so proud of them!
